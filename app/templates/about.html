About me

I am a developer from Prague, Czech Republic. My specialization is creating fast, responsive, and reliable websites. I have several years of experience in making professional, minimalistic websites that are easy to maintain and free from unnecessary dependencies.

My approach to web design (and to programming in general) is very practical. I avoid frontend frameworks when possible, prioritizing simplicity, full control over my projects, and long-term reliability.

In addition to web development, I am proficient in Python for a wide range of different applications. I have experience with machine learning (AI), data visualization, desktop app development, data processing/analysis, web scraping, automation and many more.
I usually write backends for my web applications in Python using Flask/Django.

Since I often deploy and host projects myself, I am quite knowledgeable about Linux. I can manage Linux servers on a professional level. I'm comfortable with using Docker, virtual environments etc.

You can check out some of my projects in the Projects section.
